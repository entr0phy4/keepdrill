<objective>
Stand up the `persistence/` platform seam (Dexie 4 over IndexedDB) and wire automatic,
no-explicit-save session persistence into the one existing completion boundary
(`App.tsx::handleComplete`). Every completed exercise's full raw `Session` — not just its
summary numbers — is written to IndexedDB fire-and-forget, survives a page reload, and a
save failure degrades to a small dismissible `role="status"` notice that never blocks the
results screen.

This plan LEADS with an end-to-end tracer: complete an exercise -> row in IndexedDB ->
visible in a minimal History list. Plan 04-02 expands the History list to full fidelity
(PERS-02) and hardens the D-08 hide-not-unmount contract.

Purpose: satisfies PERS-01 (auto-persist full raw session, survives reload) and PERS-03
(non-blocking save-failure notice). Establishes the Dexie schema-versioning discipline
(D-05) and the `StoredSession` on-disk record that Phases 5 and 6 read.

Output: `src/persistence/{types,db,repository}.ts` + tests, `src/test/setup-fake-indexeddb.ts`,
`src/ui/SaveFailedNotice.tsx`, a minimal `src/ui/HistoryView.tsx`, modified `src/ui/App.tsx`,
`vite.config.ts`, `package.json`, `src/index.css`.
</objective>
