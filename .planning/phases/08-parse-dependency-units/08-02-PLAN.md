- FILE-01 concurrency: sha cache + inflight; last-wins click token is 08-04.
- FILE-01 empty: empty UTF-8 blob still becomes an Exercise (normalize may append newline); PLAN-03 fallback of empty parse is 08-03.

</flagged_assumptions>
